Analysis

Two measurements on every certificate.

A purity figure with no identity confirmation answers half the question: it says the sample is clean, not that it is the compound on the label. We never issue one without the other.

Measurement 01

HPLC-UV — purity

Reversed-phase separation with ultraviolet detection at 214 nm, the wavelength at which the peptide bond absorbs. Purity is reported as area percent of the total integrated signal, to two decimal places.

Column
C18, 2.6 µm, 100 × 4.6 mm
Mobile phase
A: 0.1% TFA / H₂O — B: ACN
Gradient
5 → 65% B over 20 min
Flow / temp
1.0 mL/min, 30 °C
Detection
UV 214 nm
Reported as
Area %, 2 dp

Measurement 02

LC-MS — identity

Electrospray ionisation in positive mode. The observed mass-to-charge ratio is matched against the theoretical mass of the named sequence, including the multiply charged states expected from larger peptides.

ESI, positive mode

m/z 200 – 3000

Confirmed / Not confirmed

From vial to public record

Four steps, one of which is out of our hands.

Step 01

Sample received

Ship the vial with a completed submission form. We log it, assign an accession number, and confirm receipt by email the same day.

Step 02

Analysis

Purity and identity are run from the same vial, under the method above. No sub-contracting, no second laboratory.

Step 03

Analyst review

Peak integration and the mass match are reviewed by an analyst before anything is released. Borderline integrations get re-run.

Step 04

Report issued

The signed certificate is emailed to you and filed to this public index, where your own customers can verify it independently.

Typical turnaround: 4–6 business days from receipt.

The public record

Nothing here has to be taken on trust.

Certificates are public by default. A vendor can cite a batch; their customer can pull the same report from this page and read the chromatogram themselves.
